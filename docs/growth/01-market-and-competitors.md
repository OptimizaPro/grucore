**Licensing reality:** Alabama requires a **Home Builders Licensure Board (HBLB)** license for residential work over **$10,000** (labor + materials). Many handymen and painters can't legally take a kitchen, addition or big bath. That's a selling point once Z's license number is displayed.

### The five findings that shape the plan

1. **Nobody markets in Spanish.** Not one of ~40 sites has Spanish content, including the Hispanic-owned firms. Z is the first bilingual residential remodeler with a Spanish website in Auburn–Opelika–Phenix City.
2. **The review bar is low.** No remodeler has more than ~30 Google reviews. **30 reviews in 90 days puts Z ahead of every remodeler**; 75–100 in 12 months passes CertaPro; 125+ in 18–24 months passes E&S and Re-Bath.
3. **Nobody owns rental turnovers.** Searches for make-ready/turnover contractors in Auburn return property managers, not contractors. Auburn requires a rental business license, so landlords are businesses that want invoices, photos and speed.
4. **Nobody does build + finish.** Painters don't remodel; design-build firms don't paint cabinets or fix trim. Z does both with one crew.
5. **Paid competition is light.** Only Pythoge and JB Painting show Google Ads tags; Alabama Construction Pros runs Auburn landing pages. Clicks should be cheaper here than in bigger metros.

## Why this is winnable

Local search visibility is decided by (1) Google Business Profile category and completeness, (2) reviews — count, recency and rating, (3) proximity, and (4) a website with a dedicated page for each service. Z is based in Auburn (proximity ✓), now has the strongest service-page set in the market (✓), and competitors' review counts are low. The remaining work is the profile and the review engine — both fully in the owner's control.
