# Paid Acquisition Research: Z Construction & Remodeling LLC (Auburn, AL)

Researched 2026-09-25. Business: bilingual (EN/ES) residential remodeling, additions, kitchen and bath, painting, cabinet refinishing, flooring and repairs. Service area: Auburn, Opelika, Smiths Station, Phenix City (Lee and Russell Counties, AL). Starting budget: $1,500 to $5,000/month.

Legend: **[V]** = verified against a 2026 source (cited). **[3P]** = from third-party agency/vendor blogs, directionally useful but not official. **[UNVERIFIED]** = my inference or older knowledge that I could not confirm for 2026. Many official pages (openai.com, wordstream.com, some Meta pages) returned 403 to the fetcher, so several ChatGPT facts rest on trade-press write-ups rather than the primary source.

---

## 0. Executive summary (what to do first)

1. **Tracking before spending.** Set up GA4 + GTM, Google Ads conversions with enhanced conversions, call tracking (CallRail at $50/mo or Google forwarding numbers), a CRM with missed-call text-back, and the Meta Pixel + Conversions API. Without this you cannot tell a $90 lead from a $900 job.
2. **Google Local Services Ads (LSA) = channel #1.** Pay-per-lead, top of the page, and the "Google Verified" badge. Sign up for General Contractor, Handyman and Painter (plus Kitchen/Bath remodel if offered in the ZIPs). **Watch out: starting Oct 1, 2026, missed calls during business hours are billable** if the caller holds more than 20 seconds. So staff the phone or set hours honestly.
3. **Google Search = channel #2.** Tight, service-specific Search campaigns (exact/phrase), "Presence" location targeting, call and location assets, heavy negatives. Hold off on Performance Max until you have 30 or more conversions a month.
4. **Meta = channel #3,** mostly for retargeting and before/after creative. Remodeling and repair ads almost certainly fall under Meta's **Housing Special Ad Category** (Meta's definition includes "housing repairs"). That means a 15-mile minimum radius, no ZIP, age or gender targeting, and no lookalikes.
5. **Spanish:** Lee County is only about 5.4% Hispanic. Spanish search volume will be small, but competition is close to zero. Google is **removing campaign language targeting from Search in late Sept 2026**, so Spanish ads now match on the language of the ad text and landing page. Build a Spanish ad group and landing page, list Spanish in LSA/GBP, and use WhatsApp.
6. **ChatGPT Ads are real and self-serve,** but not a fit for a small contractor yet. Min ~$25/day, $3–5 CPC floor, no phone/call assets, no radius targeting, no geo reporting. Open the account now, and consider a capped test using the $500 match credit once Google and LSA are profitable (month 4+ in the $3k and $5k plans).

## 1. AI-assistant advertising (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Gemini)

### 1.1 ChatGPT Ads (OpenAI)

**Timeline [V/3P]**
- **Feb 9, 2026:** Ads launch for logged-in adult US users on **Free and ChatGPT Go** ($8/mo) tiers. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Education stay ad-free. Free/Go users can opt out of ads in exchange for fewer daily messages. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/09/chatgpt-ads-testing-go-free), [OpenAI approach](https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-advertising-and-expanding-access/), [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/chatgpt-ads-2026-guide))
- **Pilot:** managed-only, CPM (~$60 flat), $200K minimum, which dropped to ~$50K in April. ([Flyweel](https://www.flyweel.co/blog/openai-launches-chatgpt-ads), [Qwestyon timeline](https://www.qwestyon.com/blog/chatgpt-ads-updates))
- **May 5–6, 2026:** **Self-serve Ads Manager (beta) opens to all US businesses. No minimum spend. CPC bidding added.** Sources disagree on the exact day (May 5 vs 6). ([OpenAI "New ways to buy"](https://openai.com/index/new-ways-to-buy-chatgpt-ads/), [EasyInsights](https://easyinsights.ai/blog/openai-launches-self-serve-ads-manager-for-chatgpt/), [NateCue](https://www.natecue.com/en/news/chatgpt-self-serve-ads-manager-2026/))
- **May 22, 2026:** **State, DMA and ZIP geo-targeting** plus daily budgets. ([Wizard of Ads](https://wizardofads.org/chatgpt-ads-now-offer-geographic-targeting/), [FreeAgency](https://freeagency.ai/chatgpt-ads-manager/))
- **June–Sept 2026:** product feeds, conversion-optimized bidding (oCPC), customer-list audiences, location exclusions, carousels, view-through conversions, Europe/UK/Japan/Korea/Brazil/Mexico/India/SE Asia rollout. The platform now runs in 60+ countries with a reported ~$1B annualized run rate. ([Qwestyon](https://www.qwestyon.com/blog/chatgpt-ads-updates), [OpenAI Europe](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-across-europe/), [TechCrunch India](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/openai-to-start-showing-ads-on-chatgpts-free-and-go-tiers-in-india/))
- **Sept 16, 2026:** oCPM (impression billing optimized to conversions) is GA. Also: adjustable attribution windows (7/14/30-day click; 0/1-day view), Event Quality Score, per-platform targeting (iOS/Android app/web, desktop), **HubSpot and Shopify integrations**, AI-generated ad copy, opt-in text customization/**translation into the user's language**, and "Sponsored Agents" (US test). ([Parvi](https://parvi.ai/news/openai-chatgpt-ads-updates-september-2026/), [Qwestyon](https://www.qwestyon.com/blog/chatgpt-ads-updates))

**Formats [3P]:** A clearly labeled "Sponsored" card, visually separated at the bottom of an answer. The card shows favicon/business name, headline (~40 characters), description and link. Also shopping/product cards and carousels (feed-based), and app-install ads. **No call button, phone number, star rating, hours or map pin for local businesses.** ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/chatgpt-ads-2026-guide), [DigitalVibes](https://digitalvibes.design/blog/chatgpt-ads-for-local-business))

**Buying [3P]:**
- Self-serve Ads Manager, relevance-weighted second-price auction.
- CPC entry bids around **$3–$5**; bids under $3 "routinely fail to clear delivery thresholds."
- CPM about $25–$60.
- Reported **minimum daily budget ~$25/campaign (~$760/mo)**.
- New advertisers reported getting **$500 in matching credits for $500 spend** (reported Aug 31, 2026; offer terms fluctuate) [UNVERIFIED on official page].
- Sources: [Flyweel](https://www.flyweel.co/blog/openai-launches-chatgpt-ads), [DigitalVibes](https://digitalvibes.design/blog/chatgpt-ads-for-local-business)

**Targeting [3P]:**
- **Contextual.** You write plain-language "context hints" describing the conversations your ad should appear in. There are no keywords.
- Plus first-party signals inside OpenAI (chat history/memory, if the user allows), customer-list audiences, and geo (state/DMA/ZIP).
- **No radius targeting. No geographic breakdown in reporting.** Feed campaigns are country-level only.
- Advertisers never see chat content.
- Sources: [FreeAgency](https://freeagency.ai/chatgpt-ads-manager/), [DigitalVibes](https://digitalvibes.design/blog/chatgpt-ads-for-local-business)

**Policies [V/3P]:**
- **Prohibited:** political, adult, gambling, weapons, drugs, alcohol/tobacco, scams; legal services reportedly prohibited.
- **Restricted:** health and finance (approval required).
- Ads are never placed next to sensitive conversations (health, mental health, politics). No ads to predicted under-18 users.
- **Home improvement / general contracting is not a restricted category,** so a local contractor can advertise.
- Sources: [OpenAI Ad Policies](https://openai.com/policies/ad-policies/), [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/openai-allows-some-health-finance-ads-in-chatgpt/585516/), [AdTechRadar](https://adtechradar.com/2026/03/22/openai-chatgpt-ad-policies/)

**Measurement [3P]:**
- OpenAI pixel plus **Conversions API** (server-side). Automatic advanced matching has been on by default since Aug 2026. HubSpot integration.
- **No phone-call conversion event.** CallRail/ServiceTitan integrations aren't documented.
- Use a dedicated landing page with its own CallRail number and UTMs (`utm_source=chatgpt&utm_medium=paid-ai`).
- Sources: [FreeAgency](https://freeagency.ai/chatgpt-ads-manager/), [Qwestyon](https://www.qwestyon.com/blog/chatgpt-ads-updates)

**Assessment for Z Construction:**
- It's usable today, but the economics are poor at this budget. A $3–5 click floor, $25/day minimum, no call extension, only a small share of users see ads (Free/Go only), and there's no Auburn-level reporting.
- Contrast: local Google remodeling clicks run ~$5–14 but come with calls, maps, reviews and far more intent.
- **Recommendation:**
  - (a) Create the account now and verify the business.
  - (b) Install the OpenAI pixel via GTM (cheap to do while tagging everything else).
  - (c) Build `/chatgpt` or `/ai` landing pages (EN + ES) with a dedicated tracking number.
  - (d) Test only in month 4+ with the $500 match credit: ZIPs 36830/36832/36849/36801/36804/36867/36869/36870/36877, context hints like "planning a kitchen remodel," "bathroom renovation cost," "how much to repaint house interior," "cabinet refinishing vs replacing," with Spanish translation opt-in on.
  - (e) Kill it if cost per qualified lead is more than 2x Google Search after ~$1,000.
- **Separately, organic "AI visibility" matters more than AI ads right now.** Complete Google Business Profile + Bing Places, get reviews that mention services and cities, publish FAQ/pricing pages. ChatGPT search draws partly on Bing's index [3P: [Everything-PR](https://everything-pr.com/bing-copilot-vs-chatgpt-search-2026)].

### 1.3 Microsoft Copilot (Microsoft Advertising)
- **Copilot is a placement, not a campaign type [3P].** Regular Microsoft Advertising Search, PMax, Shopping and AI Max campaigns are automatically eligible. No opt-in is needed; conversational, long-tail and broad-match keywords help eligibility. Copilot ads run in EN/FR/DE markets, with Spanish announced.
- Showroom Ads and Offer Highlights are retail/pilot formats, not relevant here.
- Sources: [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/microsoft-advertising-copilot-strategy-2026), [Gruenberg Digital (Sept 2026)](https://www.gruenberg-digital.de/en/ki-blog/advertising-in-microsoft-copilot-2026-showroom-ads-offer-highlights.html), [Thrad](https://www.thrad.ai/content/how-to-advertise-on-microsoft-copilot)
- Microsoft CPCs are commonly 30–60% below Google, and home-services CPA runs ~$22 vs $40+ on Google [3P: [Improvado](https://improvado.io/blog/bing-ads-vs-google-ads), [NA Media](https://www.namediaexperts.com/blog-posts/microsoft-ads-vs-google-ads-2026-benchmarks)]. The audience skews older and desktop-heavy (homeowners). **Recommendation:** import the Google Search campaigns into Microsoft Ads in month 2–4 with $100–300/mo and the UET tag.
- Note: **Microsoft Ads do NOT serve inside ChatGPT.** ChatGPT ads are OpenAI's own platform [inference from sources; UNVERIFIED that no syndication deal exists].

### 1.4 Google AI Overviews / AI Mode
- Existing Search, Shopping, PMax and App ads are eligible to show above/below AI Overviews wherever AI Overviews exist. **Inside** AI Overviews and AI Mode, eligibility comes through **Performance Max, AI Max for Search, Shopping, and broad-match Search** (with Smart Bidding). ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16297775?hl=en), [Search Influence](https://www.searchinfluence.com/blog/how-ai-search-affects-paid-ads-performance-strategy-2026/))
- **Sept 4, 2026:** Google confirmed a limited test letting **exact and phrase match** keywords serve text ads in AI Mode "where there is explicit and direct user intent." AI Mode traffic is not separable in reporting. ([Miraflow](https://miraflow.ai/blog/google-ads-ai-mode-exact-phrase-match-test-2026))
- GML 2026: "Business Agent for Leads," a Gemini chat replacing static lead forms, plus conversational/"highlighted answer" formats. Rollout timing for small advertisers is unknown. ([Browser Media](https://browsermedia.agency/blog/ads-in-ai-mode-and-gml-2026-highlights/), [Google blog](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/google-marketing-live-search-ads/))
- DSA is being upgraded to AI Max. ([Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/dsa-upgrade-to-ai-max-2026/))
- The LSA "Google Verified" badge also appears in some AI Overviews [3P: [PushLeads](https://pushleads.com/google-local-service-ads-verified-badge-2026/)].
- **Recommendation:** Don't chase AI Overview placement with broad match on a small budget. Once you have 30+ tracked conversions/month and offline conversion import is working, test **one broad-match or AI Max ad group** with brand/negative lists and URL controls.

### 1.5 Gemini app
**No ads in the Gemini app** as of mid-2026. Google denied the Adweek report, and its tone has since shifted to "open-minded." ([SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-disputes-report-claiming-ads-are-coming-to-gemini-in-2026/562831/), [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-corrects-report-claiming-ads-are-coming-to-gemini-in-2026-465856), [Leapbuzz](https://leapbuzz.com/blog/does-gemini-have-ads/)) Action: none.

### 1.6 Realistic expectations for AI ads
In 2026, AI-assistant ads are a **small, experimental line item** for a local contractor. The practical wins come indirectly: Google Search/PMax ads appear around AI Overviews, and Microsoft ads appear in Copilot automatically. ChatGPT is worth ≤10% of budget as a test, only after core channels are profitable. Expect CPL at or above Google Search and weak attribution.

### 2.2 Recommended account structure (small budget)
- **Campaigns** (Search only, Google Search Network; turn OFF Display and Search Partners at first):
  1. `Brand`: "Z Construction", misspellings. Pennies per click; protects your name.
  2. `Kitchen & Bath Remodel`: ad groups for kitchen remodel, bathroom remodel, shower/tub conversion.
  3. `Painting & Cabinet Refinishing`: interior painting, exterior painting, cabinet painting/refinishing.
  4. `Flooring & Repairs / Handyman`: flooring install (LVP, tile), drywall/repair, handyman.
  5. `Additions & Remodeling (general)`: room addition, home remodeling contractor, sunroom/screened porch. Higher CPC, so run with a lower budget cap.
  6. `Español`: a Spanish ad group or campaign (see 2.4).
  - With $1,500/mo total, **merge into 2–3 campaigns** (Remodel, Paint/Refinish, Repairs+Flooring) to avoid spreading budget too thin. Aim for ≥10 clicks/day per campaign.
- **Match types:** exact and phrase. Add broad only after conversion volume exists (see 1.4).
- **Bidding:** start with Manual CPC or Maximize Clicks with a CPC cap (~$12) for 2–4 weeks. Then move to Maximize Conversions, and later tCPA once there are ≥15–30 conversions per 30 days.
- **Location:**
  - Target Auburn, Opelika, Smiths Station, Phenix City (cities plus ZIPs, or a radius around each).
  - Set **"Presence: people in or regularly in your targeted locations"** instead of the default "Presence or interest." ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722038?hl=en), [PushLeads](https://pushleads.com/local-paid-search/geo-targeting-google-ads/))
  - Exclude Columbus, GA unless you're licensed there. Georgia requires a residential contractor license for larger jobs [UNVERIFIED threshold].
- **Ad schedule:** Mon–Sat, when someone can answer the phone (e.g., 7am–7pm). After hours, run form/lead-form-only ads or use a live answering service. Bid up on weekday mornings once you have data.
- **Assets:** call asset (tracked number), location asset (linked GBP), sitelinks (Kitchen, Bath, Painting, Cabinets, Gallery, Reviews, Financing, "Hablamos Español"), callouts ("Licensed & Insured", "Free Estimates", "Bilingual EN/ES", "Local Auburn Crew"), structured snippets (Services), image assets (before/after), **lead form asset** (test it; lead forms can bring lower-quality leads, so add a qualifying question like project type/budget/timeline).
- **Landing pages:** one per service, with the phone number, a short form (name/phone/ZIP/project/timeline), before/after gallery, reviews, license/insurance, and a Spanish toggle. Speed and mobile matter: ~70% of home-service impressions are mobile [3P: BG Collective].
- **Performance Max:** **Not in months 1–6.** PMax needs 30–50 conversions/month and tends to generate spam form fills for lead gen. If tested later, use account-level negatives, brand exclusions, and offline conversion import optimized to "qualified lead/booked estimate." ([Yoshmarcom](https://yoshmarcom.com/google-performance-max-in-2026-a-complete-guide-for-lead-gen-businesses/), [Valley Marketing PMax](https://thevalleymarketinggroup.com/blog/performance-max-home-service-contractors-2026/)) Note that LSA itself is being rebuilt as a PMax-style pay-per-lead campaign type (see §3).
- **Conversion tracking:**
  - Primary conversions: form submit (thank-you page or GTM event), calls from ads ≥60s, calls from website (Google forwarding number or CallRail) ≥60s, lead-form submissions.
  - Secondary: click-to-call and click-to-WhatsApp.
  - **Enhanced conversions:** as of April 2026, EC for web and EC for leads are a single toggle. Google accepts user-provided data from tag, Data Manager or API. ([Google: EC for leads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15713840?hl=en), [Farsiight](https://www.farsiight.com/resources/offline-conversions-google-ads/))
  - **Offline conversion import:** capture GCLID (hidden form field plus CRM field). Import "Qualified Lead," "Estimate Scheduled," and "Job Won" (with value) weekly. Use **Data Manager** or a native CRM integration. API uploads moved to the Data Manager API on June 15, 2026. ([Google Ads Developer Blog](https://ads-developers.googleblog.com/2026/05/changes-to-offline-click-conversion.html), [Google: upgrade OCI](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14274408?hl=en))
  - CallRail pushes call conversions and GCLID-matched offline conversions to Google Ads automatically.

### 2.4 Spanish-language Google Ads
- **Major change:** Google is **removing campaign-level language targeting from Search campaigns (and PMax's Search inventory) in late September 2026.** Ads will match on the **language of the ad and landing page** plus Google's knowledge of the user's languages. So "set language = Spanish" is no longer a lever for Search. What matters is Spanish keywords, Spanish ad copy and a Spanish landing page. Keep each ad group in a single language. ([Google Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722078?hl=en), [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-is-removing-language-targeting-from-search-campaigns/585592/), [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ads-language-targeting-update-41867.html))
- **Costs:** Spanish keywords typically have far less competition. Google data cited as "~70% lower CPC," and some advertisers report up to 75% lower [3P: [SEJ Spanish PPC](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/spanish-ppc-keyword-research-google-ads-targeting/392777/)].
- **Market size reality:** Lee County is ~5.4% Hispanic (~9,800 people) [V: [Data USA](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/lee-county-al/)]. Many bilingual Hispanic homeowners search in English. So expect **low Spanish search volume, perhaps a handful of clicks per week** [UNVERIFIED estimate]. Run it as a low-budget ad group ($3–5/day) or fold it into the main campaigns.
- **Example Spanish keywords** (phrase/exact): "remodelación de cocina", "remodelar baño", "contratista en Auburn", "pintor de casas", "pintura de interiores", "instalación de pisos", "reparaciones de casa", "contratista que hable español", "renovación de casa Opelika", "gabinetes de cocina pintar".
- **Bilingual advantage in English ads too:** Use "Bilingual EN/ES crew" as a callout. It differentiates you and attracts landlords and property managers with Spanish-speaking tenants.

## 3. Google Local Services Ads (LSA)

**Status and changes in 2026 [V/3P]:**
- **Badges:** On Oct 20, 2025, Google replaced the Google Guaranteed, Google Screened and License Verified badges with **one blue "Google Verified" badge**. The **$2,000 money-back guarantee was discontinued.** "Google Screened" no longer exists as a separate program. ([Scorpion](https://www.scorpion.co/home-services/insights/blog/verticals/home-services/from-google-guaranteed-to-google-verified-what-h/), [Google Help](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/16498018?hl=en), [PushLeads](https://pushleads.com/google-local-service-ads-verified-badge-2026/))
- **Migration into Google Ads:** from Aug 2026, LSA is migrating into Google Ads as a new pay-per-lead **Performance Max**-based campaign type. First wave: select US home and storefront categories (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, cleaning, etc.). **Service-area businesses without storefronts follow in late 2026**; other markets and categories in 2027.
  - Manual per-lead bid caps are going away.
  - The old dashboard redirects, and historical reports don't carry over, so **export your LSA data before migration**.
  - Sources: [PinMeTo](https://www.pinmeto.com/news/google-local-services-ads-google-ads-migration-2026/), [Invoca](https://www.invoca.com/blog/googles-local-services-ads-move-to-performance-max-what-home-services-businesses-need-to-know), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-lsa-advertisers-face-missed-call-charges-from-october-1/)
- **Billing change Oct 1, 2026:** Missed calls **during stated business hours** are charged if the caller stays on the line **>20 seconds**. Qualifying follow-up calls can be charged too. Text leads are charged on send. If you use a press-1 menu, the timer starts at the key press. ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-charges-local-services-advertisers-for-missed-calls-over-20-seconds/), [Scorpion](https://www.scorpion.co/articles/topics/digital-advertising/google-is-changing-how-local-services-ads-call-l/), [Cornerstone](https://www.cornerstonead.com/2026/08/27/google-local-services-ads-changes/))
  - **Implications:** set LSA business hours to times the phone is actually answered, use an answering service or AI receptionist for overflow, and consider a short "press 1 for English, 2 para español" menu. This routes Spanish callers **and** delays the billing timer.
- **Disputes:** The manual "dispute lead" button has been replaced by AI auto-crediting of invalid leads within ~72h [3P: PushLeads]. Still mark job types and rate leads.
- **Booking:** direct-booking partners are expanding (20 → 500+). Booking is auto-enabled if GBP has a booking link [3P: PinMeTo].

**Categories:**
- Google lists 300+ categories, including **General contractor, Handyman, Painter**, carpenters and more. Some categories are state-limited. ([Google Help US](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6224841?hl=en&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DUS))
- Kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, flooring, cabinetry and similar are reported as selectable in many markets [3P: [Blue Corona](https://www.bluecorona.com/blog/google-local-services-industries/)].
- **Availability for Auburn, AL ZIPs is [UNVERIFIED]:** check at ads.google.com/local-services-ads using ZIP 36830.

## 4. Meta (Facebook / Instagram)

**Special Ad Category (Housing): likely REQUIRED.**
- Meta's Housing definition covers ads that "promote or directly link to a housing opportunity or related service, including listings for the sale or rental of a home or apartment, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, mortgage loans, **housing repairs** and home equity or appraisal services" [V via multiple quotes of Meta's text: [search results incl. Jon Loomer, Constant Contact](https://www.jonloomer.com/special-ad-categories-meta-ads/), [Constant Contact](https://www.constantcontact.com/blog/facebook-special-ad-categories/)]. Sources disagree: Data-Axle omits repairs, while Walled Garden explicitly includes home improvement.
- **Conservative stance:** run remodeling, repair and painting ads under **Housing**. Mis-categorizing risks disapprovals or account restriction.
- **Restrictions under Housing (US):**
  - No age or gender targeting.
  - **Minimum 15-mile radius**, and no ZIP targeting.
  - Detailed targeting is heavily limited.
  - **No lookalikes.**
  - Custom audiences (website visitors, video viewers, customer lists) are allowed.
  - Sources: [Walled Garden](https://walledgardenhq.com/blog/special-ad-category-real-estate), [Data-Axle](https://www.data-axle.com/resources/blog/meta-special-ad-categories-rules/)
- **Language targeting under Housing** is [UNVERIFIED]. Target Spanish speakers through **Spanish creative, copy and a Spanish lead form**, not ethnicity. Meta also removed "multicultural affinity" targeting years ago, so never target by ethnicity.
- **Geo tactic:** use three location pins (Auburn, Opelika, Phenix City) with 15-mile radii. The Phenix City pin spills into Columbus, GA: add "Serving Lee & Russell County, AL" in the copy and a service-area question in the form.

**Benchmarks:**
- LocaliQ 2026 (Home & Home Improvement, lead campaigns): **CTR 2.14%, CPC $2.18, CVR 5.32%, CPL $42.95** [V: [LocaliQ FB benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/facebook-advertising-benchmarks/)].
- Remodeling-specific Meta CPL is often **$80–$120** [3P: [AdManage](https://admanage.ai/blog/facebook-ads-cost-per-lead-benchmarks), [TheAdSpend](https://theadspend.com/blog/facebook-leads-home-improvement)].
- Expect lower intent and lower close rates than search.

**Lead forms vs landing pages:**
- Native Instant Forms usually give 20–60% lower CPL but lower quality. ([Barham](https://barhammarketing.com/meta-lead-forms-vs-landing-pages-12-pros-and-cons-to-consider-2026/), [AdAmigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/meta-lead-form-vs-landing-page-benchmarks-by-industry-2026))
- **Recommendation:** use **"Higher intent" Instant Forms** with 2–3 qualifying questions (project type; budget range; timeline; city/ZIP; preferred language EN/ES) plus a review screen.
- Sync forms instantly to the CRM (Zapier, LeadsBridge, or native Jobber/HCP/GHL integrations) so the 5-minute text/call fires.
- Test a landing-page variant for kitchens/baths/additions.

**Creatives that work (industry consensus [3P]):**
- Before/after carousels and slider reels
- 15–30s Reels: time-lapse, owner walk-through in English and a Spanish version
- "Day in the life" crew clips
- Customer testimonial selfie-video
- Cabinet refinishing transformations (very visual, lower ticket; a strong entry offer)
- Seasonal offers ("Fall interior painting slots open")
- Financing messaging

Keep the brand/phone/logo on screen, and use captions (most viewing is muted).

**Other Meta tools:**
- Click-to-WhatsApp / click-to-Messenger ads (good for Spanish speakers)
- Retargeting website visitors and video viewers for 30–90 days (cheap, high-return)
- Boosted before/after posts in local groups are not possible, but you can post organically in Auburn/Opelika community groups and Spanish-language community groups

**Speed-to-lead:**
- Leads contacted within **5 minutes are ~21x more likely to qualify** than those contacted after 30 minutes (MIT/InsideSales). HBR (2011) found contact within 1 hour gives ~7x the qualification odds. The average business takes 42–47 hours. ([Kixie](https://www.kixie.com/sales-blog/speed-to-lead-response-time-statistics-that-drive-conversions/), [LeadAngel](https://www.leadangel.com/blog/operations/speed-to-lead-statistics/))
- On shared-lead platforms (Thumbtack/Angi) and LSA ranking, responsiveness directly affects wins and ranking.
- **SOP:**
  1. Instant auto-text in the lead's language: "Hi {name}, this is {owner} with Z Construction — got your {project} request. What's a good time for a quick call? / Hola {nombre}, soy {owner} de Z Construction…"
  2. Call within 5 minutes during business hours.
  3. Missed call → auto text-back within 60 seconds.
  4. Follow-up cadence: day 0, 1, 3, 7, 14.
  5. After the estimate: follow up at 2, 7 and 21 days.
- **Review automation:** Send an SMS/email review request at job completion with a direct GBP review link, in both languages, with one reminder after 3 days. Don't gate reviews or incentivize them; that violates Google policy.

**Texting compliance (TCPA / A2P 10DLC):**
- **A2P 10DLC registration is mandatory** for business texting from local numbers. Unregistered traffic has been blocked by carriers since Feb 2025. Register the Brand (1–3 days) and Campaign use-case (2–7 days) through your provider (Jobber/HCP/GHL/Twilio/CallRail all handle this). ([Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/blog/tcpa-compliance-sms), [Beancount](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/06/04/tcpa-a2p-10dlc-sms-marketing-compliance-2026-small-business-text-program-the-campaign-registry-consent-stop-help-keyword-state-mini-tcpa-florida-ftsa-texas-sb140-guide))
- **Consent:** Replying to a person who texted or called you, or who submitted a form asking to be contacted, is generally a conversational/informational message. **Marketing texts** (promos, seasonal offers) need **prior express written consent**. Add an unchecked-by-default SMS consent checkbox on forms with clear disclosure ("Msg & data rates may apply, reply STOP to opt out"), in English and Spanish.
- **Opt-outs:** Honor opt-outs made **by any reasonable means** ("stop," "no more texts," "no me escriba") within **10 business days**; do it immediately if possible. ([IDT Express](https://www.idtexpress.com/blog/tcpa-compliance-for-sms-in-2026-the-complete-guide-for-us-businesses/))
- Quiet hours: 8am–9pm recipient local time. Some state mini-TCPAs are stricter; Alabama has none of the Florida/Texas type [UNVERIFIED].
- Keep consent logs with timestamp, source form and IP. The CRM should store them.
- **WhatsApp:** The Business app is fine for 1:1 conversations. Bulk marketing needs the WhatsApp Business Platform/API with opt-in and paid template messages [UNVERIFIED current pricing].
