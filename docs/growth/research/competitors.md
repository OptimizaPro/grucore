# Competitive Research: Z Construction & Remodeling LLC (Auburn, AL)

Research date: 2026-09-25. Method: web search, Houzz, Birdeye, BBB, GuildQuality and Nextdoor directory pages, plus direct HTML audits of competitor homepages (title, meta, schema, platform, city mentions, internal links, ad tags). Google Maps, Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor and CertaPro blocked automated fetches (403/JS). So **Google review counts come from Birdeye mirrors where available** (Birdeye shows the Google count at the time it was captured). Everything else is marked **(unverified)**. Google Local Services Ads / "Google Guaranteed" badges can't be seen without a live Google search, so LSA status is **unverified for every competitor**. The only ad evidence below is Google Ads conversion tags (AW-xxxx) found in page source.

## 3. Gaps / white space Z Construction can own

1. **Bilingual (English/Spanish) residential contractor.** Not one competitor audited has Spanish content, a Spanish page or a "Se habla español" badge, including the Hispanic-owned ones (Peruvian Brothers, and possibly E&S and Morado). Lee County is about **5.2–5.7% Hispanic (~9,100–9,800 people)**, up from 1.4% in 2000 ([Wikipedia/Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_County,_Alabama), [Data USA](https://datausa.io/profile/geo/lee-county-al/)). A Spanish Mass runs weekly at St. Michael the Archangel, Auburn (Sun 12:30) with St. Mary's Opelika's Hispanic ministry ([St. Mary Opelika – Español](https://stmaryopelika.org/espanol)). **Z can own every Spanish-language query in Lee County and the Columbus area outright**: "contratista en Auburn AL", "remodelación de cocinas Opelika", "pintores en Auburn", "remodelación de baños", and so on. It can also sell to English-speaking owners who hire Spanish-speaking crews ("your whole crew and your owner speak both languages").
2. **Build + finish under one roof.** Painters don't remodel, and design-build firms don't take small painting, cabinet or repair jobs. Z can position as "one licensed contractor from framing to final coat": additions, kitchens and baths, **then** paint, cabinets and flooring, with no handoffs. Only JL Remodeling and Intelligent Design are near this, and neither markets it strongly.
3. **Rental turnover / make-ready for Auburn University landlords and out-of-town owners.** A search for "make ready / rental turnover contractor Auburn" turned up **no dedicated contractor**, only property managers (Wade, Liberty, 360, Auburn Realty). Auburn University hit **a record 35,172 students in fall 2025** (28,953 undergrads, seventh straight record) ([Yellowhammer](https://yellowhammernews.com/auburn-university-sets-new-fall-enrollment-record-driven-by-in-state-student-surge/)). Every rental in city limits needs a residential rental business license (1.5% of gross receipts after year one) ([City of Auburn](https://www.auburnal.gov/revenue/business-licenses/residential-rental/)), so owners are formal businesses that want invoices, photos and speed. Offer: fixed-price turnover packages (paint, patch, flooring, fixtures, punch list), a May–August turnover calendar, photo/video reports for parents who own condos, and property-manager pricing. Only Auburn Handyman Service mentions investors or absentee owners, on a 4-page Weebly site.
4. **Transparent pricing.** Only Old Number One publishes cost ranges, and they're premium. Alabama Construction Pros offers an "instant estimate." Z can publish **starting-at prices for mid-market work**: bath refresh, interior repaint per room, cabinet painting per kitchen, turnover packages. None of the painters show prices.
5. **Owner on site, family-owned.** The premium firms sell "the founder," and the franchises sell "process." Z can pitch "the owner is on every job and answers his own phone, in English or Spanish." Pair it with real photos and a crew/owner video.
6. **The towns nobody targets with pages.** Triple B and Nailed It! mention Loachapoka, Beauregard, Waverly, Notasulga and Salem only in homepage text. **Nobody has dedicated city pages for Smiths Station, Beauregard, Salem, Waverly, Notasulga or Loachapoka.** E&S has service × city pages only for Auburn, Opelika and Dadeville. Z can build service × city pages (EN + ES) for all 9 service areas.
7. **Warranty and financing parity.** Among locals, only Old Number One (1-yr) and R Lucas (1-yr) state workmanship warranties. Financing appears only with franchises, Alabama Construction Pros and Kirkley. A written 2-year workmanship warranty plus a financing option (e.g., Hearth/Wisetack) would match the franchises and beat the locals.
8. **Cabinet refinishing as a standalone product.** Kitchen Tune-Up Columbus and N-Hance appear absent, which leaves painters doing it as a side service. A dedicated cabinet page with before/after photos and a price range is open ground.
9. **Historic-home work.** TLC won a 2025 Historic Remodel award, and Alabama Construction Pros has a historic-remodeling guide. Z could add a page on COA-compliant exterior painting and repairs for Opelika's Northside/Geneva Street districts and Auburn's Old Main/Church St area (see section 6). That's a small but high-trust niche.

## 6. Auburn/Opelika facts useful for marketing (verified unless marked)

**Licensing**
- **Alabama Home Builders Licensure Board:** a residential home builder license is required when "the cost of the undertaking including labor and materials exceeds $10,000" for construction, remodeling, repair or improvement of residential structures ([HBLB FAQ](https://hblb.alabama.gov/faqs-applicants/), [HBLB law](https://hblb.alabama.gov/law/)). Marketing angle: "State-licensed for projects over $10,000." Many handymen and painters can't legally take a $10k+ kitchen or addition.
- The City of Auburn says residential construction requires an HBLB Residential Homebuilder license, and **residential roofing over $2,500 needs state licensure** ([Auburn – Do I need a permit](https://www.auburnal.gov/inspection-services/do-i-need-a-permit/)).
- Insurance: HBLB law "does not address insurance" per the FAQ. Third-party guides cite $100k/occurrence GL for license issuance (unverified; [LegalClarity](https://legalclarity.org/how-to-get-an-alabama-home-builders-license/)).

**Permits: City of Auburn** (Inspection Services, 171 N. Ross St, (334) 501-3170, plans@auburnal.gov, Auburn Permit Portal) ([Auburn Inspection Services](https://www.auburnal.gov/inspection-services/), [Applications](https://www.auburnal.gov/inspection-services/services/applications/))
- Permit **required**: additions, interior alterations (moving walls, kitchen and bath remodels), decks (new or repair), siding and windows, roofing, accessory structures, retaining walls over 4 ft, fences over 7 ft, piping changes, water heaters, most electrical, HVAC/ductwork.
- **No permit**: painting, carpet, trim, fixture swaps without piping changes, electrical fixture repair without new circuits.
- Inspections need one day's notice. The third inspection of the same phase costs $35, and any further ones $120 each ([Required Inspections](https://www.auburnal.gov/inspection-services/services/required-inspections/)).

**Permits: City of Opelika** (Building Inspection Division, 710 Fox Trail) ([Opelika residential permit process](https://www.opelika-al.gov/665/Residential-Building-Permit-Process))
- Plans show heated sq ft, porches and garages, and fees are based on contract cost. Approval takes 3–4 business days. Sewer assessment fees are due before the permit. **The permit poster must be posted on site or no inspections happen.** Electrical, plumbing and mechanical subs pull their own permits.
- Unincorporated Lee County (e.g., Beauregard, Smiths Station outskirts, Salem, Loachapoka) is handled by [Lee County Building Inspection](https://www.leeco.us/departments/building_inspection/index.php). Smiths Station is its own city; permit specifics unverified.

**Historic districts**
- **Opelika:** a Certificate of Appropriateness is required for exterior changes in the **Northside Historic District** (designated 2000; roughly 7th Ave / 3rd St / 3rd Ave / 11th St; Victorian, Neoclassical, Mission Revival, 1880s–1940s), the **Downtown Historic District** and the **Geneva Street Historic District** ([Opelika COA](https://www.opelika-al.gov/395/Certificate-of-Appropriateness-COA), [Historic Preservation](https://www.opelika-al.gov/169/Historic-Preservation)).
- **Auburn:** Historic Preservation Commission COAs are required for alterations that change design, materials or appearance in local districts. The **North College Historic District** (Ord. 2377, 2006) sits inside the NRHP-listed **Old Main and Church Street Historic District** (1978) ([Auburn Comp Plan ch. 9](https://static.auburnalabama.org/media/apps/www/compplan2030/9.0-Historic-Preservation.pdf)).

**Rentals and the university**
- Auburn University fall 2025 enrollment: **35,172 total (record, 7th straight year)** ([Yellowhammer](https://yellowhammernews.com/auburn-university-sets-new-fall-enrollment-record-driven-by-in-state-student-surge/)).
- City of Auburn residential rental business license: $105 in year one, then 1.5% of prior-year gross rental receipts. Short-term rentals under 180 days need a general business license plus lodging tax ([Auburn Residential Rental](https://www.auburnal.gov/revenue/business-licenses/residential-rental/), [Short-term rentals](http://www.auburnal.gov/short-term-rentals/)).
- Student-housing managers to court for turnover work: Wade Management ([waderentals.com](https://www.waderentals.com/)), Liberty Properties, 360 Management Services, Auburn Realty.
