# 02 · Positioning & offers

> **Z Construction & Remodeling es la empresa familiar de Auburn que lo construye *y* lo termina — una sola cuadrilla, un precio por escrito y el dueño en cada obra, en español o en inglés.**

Use these in ads, posts, yard signs and on the phone. Keep the wording consistent everywhere — AI assistants and Google both reward a consistent story.

## Offers to launch (owner decides)

Offers give ads something concrete to say and give people a reason to act now. Each is a business decision — add them to the site only once you're committed.

| Offer | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Written 2-year workmanship warranty** | Only two locals state any warranty (1 year); franchises offer 2. Matching them removes an objection. | Put it in the estimate template first, then on the site. |
| **Financing** (e.g., Hearth, Wisetack, GreenSky via a lender) | Franchises and Alabama Construction Pros advertise it; "kitchen remodel near me with financing" is a real search. | Merchant fees ~0–10%; price accordingly. |
| **Cabinet Refresh Package** — "painted cabinets + new hardware + soft-close hinges, from $X" | Lowest-risk entry product; highly visual for Facebook/Instagram. | Great first job that leads to counters/backsplash. |
| **Tub-to-Shower Conversion, from $X** | High "near me" search demand; aging-in-place homeowners. | Productize: fixed scope, 3 tile/glass tiers. |
| **Rental Turnover Packages** (Light / Medium / Heavy per unit) with **spring booking discount** | Fills the calendar before the August rush; locks in landlords. | Offer net-15/30 to property managers. |
| **Neighbor referral**: $150 gift card or local charity donation per referred *completed* job | Word of mouth is how the Hispanic community and HOAs choose. | Mail a referral card with every final invoice. |
| **Free "Pre-listing punch list" walkthrough for realtors** | Realtors control a steady stream of inspection repairs. | 48-hour written quotes. |

## Proof assets to collect (every job, starting now)

- Before / during / after photos from the same angles (horizontal + vertical for Reels).
- A 20-second phone video of the finished room.
- The review request, sent the day the job closes (see [07](07-lead-system.md)).
- A one-line case study: *"1998 kitchen in Yarbrough Farms — painted cabinets, quartz, new lighting — 9 days — $16k."* These become GBP posts, Our Work entries, and the "real projects" that make town pages rank.

## Words to avoid

- "Cheap", "best prices", "lowest bid" — attracts the wrong jobs and is what the ads' negative keywords filter out.
- Anything about immigration status, ethnicity-based targeting, or "Hispanic prices" — speak to *language*, never ethnicity.
- Claims you can't document ("#1 in Auburn", "100+ five-star reviews") until they're true.
